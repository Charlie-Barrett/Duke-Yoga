# Duke Yoga - search demand research (DataForSEO, live, Sept 2026)

Volumes below are searches made **from within Worcestershire** (county-level targeting), not national figures. That is the demand Fiona can actually win.

## 1. Local demand concentrates in "near me", not town names

| Keyword (searched from Worcestershire) | Vol/mo | Competition |
|---|---|---|
| yoga classes near me | 210 (spikes to 320) | LOW |
| yoga near me | 170 (spikes to 320) | LOW |
| vinyasa yoga | 70 | LOW |
| hatha yoga | 50 | LOW |
| chair yoga (+ for seniors + near me) | ~80 combined | **HIGH (ad competition 99–100)** |
| yoga for beginners (+ near me) | ~50 | LOW–MED |
| yoga classes | 30 | LOW |
| yoga worcester / droitwich / malvern, private lessons, workshops | below reporting threshold | - |

Implications: town-name keywords are too small to build pages around; "near me" queries (the bulk of demand) are resolved by **proximity + Google Business Profile**, not on-page keywords. The website's job is to convert, answer questions, and carry venue/area signals - GBP does the ranking. Chair yoga's HIGH ad competition despite modest volume means advertisers find those clicks valuable: it's the most commercially contested term in the set, and Duke Yoga has almost no local competition organically for it.

## 2. Seasonality - where demand spikes

County monthly data (12 months): **January** (+25–75% across all terms; chair yoga nearly doubles to 70) and **September/October** (back-to-routine; "yoga near me" hit 320 in Sept). December is the trough. Site now includes a seasonal banner slot in `index.html`; time Facebook pushes, workshop announcements and any ad spend for the first two weeks of January and the first two weeks of September.

## 3. What the live Worcester SERP rewards ("yoga classes worcester")

- **Local pack sits above all organic results.** Won by Yoga 7 (51 reviews, 5.0), The Hot Tribe, Balance Cafe & Yoga (57 reviews). Duke Yoga is absent. Review count + a complete GBP is the single biggest ranking lever available.
- Organic winners are small practitioner sites with location-rich titles - including Worcester Wellness ranking with a plain **timetable page** that lists "Chair Yoga – Gentle movers". Timetable pages rank; ours is structured for it.
- Directories (ThreeBestRated, Yell, Cylex, Tripadvisor) hold a top "find results on" block - free citations worth claiming.
- **People Also Ask** (all with AI Overviews): free yoga in Worcester, beginner classes, average cost, best style for beginners. The new Beginners page answers each one directly with FAQPage schema - targeting PAA and AI Overview citation.
- Related searches: "Free yoga classes worcester", "Beginner yoga classes worcester" - Duke's free first session is the literal answer to the top related search; the site now says so explicitly.

## 4. What changed in v3 (vs v2)

1. **New `/beginners` page** - targets beginner + free + cost intent, carries FAQPage structured data for PAA/AI Overviews. Added to nav, sitemap.
2. **Chair yoga repositioned** as "Chair Yoga for Seniors & Gentle Movers" - matches "chair yoga for seniors" (highest-competition local term) and the January spike.
3. **Seasonal banner slot** on the home page with usage instructions (Jan/Sept).
4. **Venue areas named up front** on the Classes page (Droitwich Spa, Crowle, Whittington, Worcester area) - the proximity signal we control on-site.
5. **Workshops copy names yin yoga and yoga nidra** (9,900 UK searches/mo each) instead of describing them generically.

## 5. The plan the website can't do for you (ranked by impact)

1. **Google Business Profile**: create/claim for Duke Yoga, category "Yoga studio", service area Droitwich/Worcester, all four venues' class times, photos, and a steady drip of review requests to long-term students. This is the whole ballgame for the 380+/mo "near me" cluster.
2. **Citations**: get listed on Yell, Cylex, ThreeBestRated, and Tripadvisor (all on page 1 for the money term).
3. **Reviews**: 6 website testimonials, 0 visible Google reviews vs competitors' 50+. Ask the six people already quoted on the site to repost on Google.
4. **Prices on the site**: PAA asks "average cost", competitors publish prices (£12.50 at Studio Three). The site currently says "contact for prices" - publishing them would convert better and is worth pressing Fiona on.
