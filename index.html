Before you ask

First-timer questions

I’m not flexible at all. Can I still come?

That’s exactly why you come — flexibility is the result of yoga, not the entry requirement. Every pose is adapted to the body it’s in.

Am I too old for this?

No. Classes span every age and ability, and the Monday chair yoga class means even limited mobility isn’t a barrier. Everyone is welcome, no exceptions.

What do I need to bring?

Comfy clothes you can move in, water, and a blanket for relaxation. Bring a mat if you have one — spares are available. For chair yoga, just bring yourself.

Completely new to yoga?

Start here: our beginners page covers your first class, what it costs, and which class to pick.

How much does it cost?

Your first session is completely free, so you can try before you commit. Message Fiona for current class prices.

Do I have to book?

It helps! Drop Fiona a line or message on Facebook and she’ll save you a spot.

Your first class is free.

No experience, no bendiness, no special kit required. Just come as you are.
