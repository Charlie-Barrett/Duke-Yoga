Seated yoga · Mondays 10–11am

Chair yoga for seniors & gentle movers: all the benefit, none of the floor

Better mobility, strength, balance and calm — all from the support of a chair. No getting up and down, no experience needed, no one left behind.

Who it’s for

Anyone with limited mobility, joint pain or balance concerns; older adults; people recovering from injury or illness; and anyone who finds mat-based classes intimidating. Especially popular with the over-60s — and January fills fast.

What to expect

Gentle stretches, strength work and breathing exercises, all seated or supported by the chair. Every movement adapts to you. We finish with a proper relaxation.

What to bring

Just yourself and comfortable clothing. Chairs are provided — and your first session is completely free.

When & where

Mondays, 10–11am at The Herriots. Weekly, year-round. Get in touch to check availability and claim your free first session.
